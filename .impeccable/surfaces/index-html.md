# Artvue marketplace

## Scope

Home, browse, artwork, artist, about, sell, and credits. Mode: Persuade on the home page (the visit should end in a WhatsApp inquiry) and Operate on browse, artwork, and artist. The home page is the primary surface.

## Audience and job

A visitor decides whether a fictional work is worth a WhatsApp message. They need the picture, the lot line, and the inquiry in one reading.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An auction-house catalogue on ivory paper. The painting is full-bleed and large; the type sits below it like a lot description. Refuses the white startup grid, the cream-and-terracotta template, and any overlay that competes with the canvas.

OWN-WORLD: Daylight in a quiet room. Ground is ivory. Ink is charcoal. A brass line is used once, as metal, not as a theme. Display is EB Garamond italic. Catalogue facts are a plain sans. Rules are one pixel. Cards have no shadow and no chrome beyond the picture and the lot line. Motion is a single slow settle of the opening image.

STORY: The visitor meets a real painting at wall scale, understands the demo, browses by category, style, medium, price, size, and orientation, and sends a WhatsApp note that names the piece.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ivory bar with the Artvue wordmark, category links, saved works, and contact. A one-line demo notice. Then a full-bleed painting, about 78vh, with no text on the picture. Under it, on ivory, a large serif heading, the lot line for the painting just shown, and two actions: view the work, browse the catalogue. At 360px the painting is about 62vh and the heading wraps under it. No eyebrow above the heading.

FORM: Client-pinned auction-house register (ivory, charcoal, spare brass, serif display, full-bleed paintings). Supersedes the earlier white-gallery note. Seed key: client-pinned-luxury.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
