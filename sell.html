Sell your art

Artvue is not taking applications into a live market. This page shows how an artist would ask to be included. There is no form and no account. The note goes to WhatsApp.

What to send

Your name, the city you work in, a link if you have one, and three works with medium and size. A sentence on what you want the work to do in a room is enough.

What this demo will not do

It will not store the message, invoice a buyer, or ship a crate. The number on the button is the only contact, and it belongs to this demonstration.
